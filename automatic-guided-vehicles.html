Advantages of AGVs vs Traditional Forklift Trucks

Precise & Safe Operation

Predictability of AGVs; routes remain unchanged over time. Operation 24/7 with no human intervention required. Elimination of the risks associated with use of forklift trucks. No damage caused by AGVs to goods, machinery, or permanent structures.

Easy-to-Assemble & Maintain

Thanks to the 'all plugs' feature, all components are connected using connectors that do not require the use of a screwdriver. This allows very easy and quick assembly or replacement of parts.

Advanced Interface

Quick and easy programming by means of a touch panel or PC interface. The software interface is designed to be self-explanatory and does not require long training.

Higher Speed & Stability

A closed loop control system enables precise tracking of the magnet guide tape without deviation or variation, guaranteeing safety on turns.

Flexibility

No conventional materials-handling infrastructure required. The number of AGVs can be increased as business grows. Routes are easy to reconfigure.

Optimum Quality-to-Price Ratio

Low running and maintenance costs. Average year cost over a 5-year period for 5 AGVs is about 1/3 of the average cost for 2 forklift trucks.
