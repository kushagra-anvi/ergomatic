INDEVA electronic range

Liftronic® intelligent manipulators

Liftronic is INDEVA’s electronic manipulator range, a separate product technology from ERGOMATIC electro-pneumatic systems. These intelligent assist devices use electronic control and are designed for applications requiring responsive movement and precise load positioning.

Liftronic® Pro

For custom handling applications, including load rotation, vacuum gripping, and special handling controls. Officially listed models range from 80 to 320 kg including the end effector; mounting options include column, ceiling, overhead rail or bridge crane, and Liftruck.

Liftronic® Easy

Electronic lift-assist manipulator with standard gripping tools.

Liftronic® Air

Parallelogram rigid arm for the vertical axis, suitable for handling offset loads.

Liftronic® Mobile

Battery-powered mobile solution designed to move between workstations.

INDEVA electronic handling features

  • Automatic weight sensing and balancing
  • Responsive, precise movement for ergonomic load handling
  • Remote troubleshooting through App-Indeva
  • Industry 4.0 interconnection capability and system diagnostics
  • Standard or application-specific end effectors
