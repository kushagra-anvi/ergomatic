Who We Are

INDEVA Ergomatic India Private Limited is a leading provider of advanced industrial manipulators and material handling automation solutions.

Formed through a strategic joint venture between Scaglia INDEVA S.p.A. (a global pioneer in ergonomic automation headquartered in Italy) and Ergomatic Automation India Pvt. Ltd. (known for its strong local engineering expertise and customer network), our company combines international cutting-edge technology with deep Indian market understanding.

Our focus lies in designing and implementing premium ergonomic equipment and automation systems that meet rigorous international standards, particularly in difficult and dangerous environments where reliability and safety are paramount.

JV Foundations

Scaglia INDEVA S.p.A. has been at the forefront of the design and manufacture of industrial manipulators since 1970. Today, they are the world leader in intelligent assist devices (IADs).

Ergomatic Automation India brings robust local engineering capability, customized tooling design, manufacturing facilities in Pune, and a strong sales & support framework.
