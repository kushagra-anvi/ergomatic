About Our Brands

Three Specialized Solutions, One Ecosystem

ERGOMATIC (India-focused) specializes in Pneumatic & Electro-Pneumatic manipulators engineered for the Indian market. INDEVA® (Italy-based, premium) provides intelligent electronic lifting systems with advanced controls. SYSDESIGN® delivers autonomous robots and AGV solutions for Industry 4.0 smart logistics.

Formed through a strategic joint venture between Scaglia INDEVA S.p.A. (Italy) and Ergomatic Automation India Pvt. Ltd., we combine international innovation with deep local market expertise. With over 100 years of combined pedigree, we deliver solutions that enhance safety, productivity, and ergonomics.
